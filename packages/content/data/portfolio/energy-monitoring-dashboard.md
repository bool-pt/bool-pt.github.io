## The Challenge

A national energy utility lacked real-time visibility into grid performance across its network. Data lived in siloed SCADA systems, and operational teams could not identify inefficiencies or predict failures before they occurred.

## The Solution

A custom-built monitoring dashboard using React, Node.js, and time-series databases, ingesting data from thousands of IoT sensors across the grid. Machine learning models predict equipment failures and optimise load distribution in real time.

## Key Results

- **Real-time monitoring** of 15,000+ grid points
- **35% reduction** in unplanned outages
- **20% operational cost savings** through predictive maintenance
- **Unified view** replacing 6 legacy monitoring tools
