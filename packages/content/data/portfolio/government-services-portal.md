## The Challenge

Citizens had to visit physical offices for routine administrative tasks — licence renewals, permit applications, and benefit requests. Wait times averaged 45 minutes, and staff spent most of their time on repetitive data entry.

## The Solution

A Microsoft Power Platform-based portal providing end-to-end digital services. Citizens authenticate securely, submit requests, upload documents, and receive automated status updates. Back-office workflows route cases to the right department and track SLAs.

## Key Results

- **500,000+ citizens** using the platform
- **80% reduction** in in-person visits for routine services
- **Average processing time** cut from 15 days to 3 days
- **Staff redeployed** to higher-value citizen support roles
