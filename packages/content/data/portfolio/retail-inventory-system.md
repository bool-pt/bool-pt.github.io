## The Challenge

An international retail chain with 200+ stores struggled with inventory accuracy. Stockouts cost millions in lost revenue, while overstock tied up capital. Store managers relied on gut feeling rather than data for replenishment decisions.

## The Solution

An OutSystems-powered inventory management system with AI-driven demand forecasting. The platform integrates with POS systems and supplier APIs to automate replenishment orders based on real-time sales velocity, seasonality, and local demand patterns.

## Key Results

- **55% reduction** in stockouts across all stores
- **30% decrease** in overstock levels
- **Automated replenishment** for 85% of SKUs
- **ROI achieved** within 8 months of deployment
