## The Challenge

Fragmented credit risk processes across multiple markets led to inconsistent assessments, manual bottlenecks, and regulatory compliance gaps. Each regional team relied on disconnected spreadsheets and legacy systems, making it impossible to get a unified view of credit exposure.

## The Solution

A unified low-code application built on Mendix, automating core credit risk workflows from application intake to final decision. The platform integrates with existing core banking systems via REST APIs and provides real-time dashboards for risk managers across all markets.

## Key Results

- **60% reduction** in credit assessment processing time
- **Unified risk view** across 5 European markets
- **Full regulatory compliance** with Basel III reporting requirements
- **200+ users** onboarded within the first quarter
