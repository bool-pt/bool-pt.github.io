## The Challenge

A major retail operation handling thousands of daily orders relied on manual processes and disconnected systems for inventory, fulfilment, and customer communication. Order errors were frequent and customer satisfaction was declining.

## The Solution

An OutSystems-based order management platform that connects e-commerce, warehouse management, and logistics into a single real-time workflow. Automated validation, intelligent routing, and proactive customer notifications transformed the entire order lifecycle.

## Key Results

- **45% faster** order fulfilment
- **92% reduction** in order errors
- **Real-time visibility** across 12 distribution centres
- **30% improvement** in customer satisfaction scores
