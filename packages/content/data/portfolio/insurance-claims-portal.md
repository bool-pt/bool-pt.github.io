## The Challenge

Policyholders faced a frustrating claims experience: paper forms, long wait times, and no visibility into claim status. Internal teams were overwhelmed with manual data entry and follow-up calls.

## The Solution

A self-service claims portal built on OutSystems, enabling policyholders to submit claims, upload documents, and track progress in real time. AI-powered document classification and automated routing accelerate processing while reducing human error.

## Key Results

- **70% reduction** in claims processing time
- **85% of claims** submitted through self-service
- **40% fewer** follow-up calls to support
- **Net Promoter Score** increased by 25 points
