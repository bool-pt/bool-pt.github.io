06Success Stories

OutSystems delivery for enterprise clients.

Small, senior teams solving problems that sit at the heart of the business.

Energy cost predictionEnergy · United States

Station delivery costs were hard to forecast, and teams relied on gut instinct to estimate them. We built an app to store and edit pipeline fuel flow rates, energy and component costs, using historical operational data to predict the cost of future fuel deliveries at every station.

The benefits

  • Improved profit margins: accurate cost predictions support better pricing at each station.
  • Operational efficiency: knowing the real cost drivers helps optimize schedules, routes and maintenance.
  • Data-driven planning: better seasonal forecasts and long-term decisions on network expansion.
OutSystems 11 · Data platform integration · 1 Tech Lead, 1 Developer, 1 Business Analyst
E-returns systemRetail · Netherlands

Online purchases can see return rates of 20 to 40%, against under 10% in store. We built a centralized returns web app with role-specific workflows for warehouse, front office, back office and finance, including refund workflows and a financial dashboard.

The benefits

  • Faster processing: no manual handoffs, return-to-refund time cut from days to hours.
  • Financial control: full visibility of refund costs, fraud prevention through approvals and accurate forecasting.
  • Inventory accuracy: real-time stock updates across distribution centers as returns are registered.
