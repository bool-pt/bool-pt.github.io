Who we are

Bool, founded in 2013, is part of the Code for All_ Group: a software consultancy delivering critical custom solutions from Portugal to the world.

Over a decade of hands-on experience across the full application lifecycle, from Ideation and Architecture to Development, Training, and beyond. We work side by side with clients, delivering results that compound.

Mission

Bool exists to build software that makes a real difference. Part of the Code for All_ Group, we are the reference for critical software delivery — fast, reliable, and driven by measurable impact. We earn that trust every day by getting it done.

Vision

Our vision is a world where technology leads the change it promises. At Bool, we make that concrete: world-class talent, leading technology stacks, and a culture that elevates everyone — teammates, clients, and partners — toward outcomes that genuinely matter.
