Case study · AI-Augmented Development · Retail technology · Italy

A certified fiscal integration, built in 11 days

11 daysfrom accepted proposal to working integration
6 weeksBool’s estimate for the same work without AI
0 defectsin production, through the Winter Olympics and Paralympics

The challenge

Cashierless stores in Italy had to register every sale under Italian fiscal law, through a certified provider, by a fixed mid December deadline. The provider asked for payment data the client did not hold.

How we built it

A week of working sessions with the client and the provider mapped the API and the data. One Bool developer and an AI agent then built it: the agent wrote and tested the code, the developer designed and reviewed it.

How the work ran

  1. 1Developer designs the integration
  2. 2Agent writes the code
  3. 3Agent tests it against the fiscal API
  4. 4Developer reviews and owns each change

Case study · Our product · Accounting · Portugal

Invoices into the ERP, a person approving every record

About 40%of 1,400 documents read without AI
1.2 centsaverage cost per document in production
0 recordssaved without a person’s approval

The challenge

Accountants retype invoices into the ERP and match bank statements by hand.

How we built it

Conto AI reads the Portuguese ATCUD code first, at no cost and with no AI. Only damaged photos, scans and foreign documents go to AI reading. Deterministic where it can be, AI where it must be.

How the work runs

  1. 1Read the ATCUD code, with no AI
  2. 2AI reads only what the code cannot
  3. 3The product proposes the record and the bank match
  4. 4A person approves before anything is saved

0personal data of children sent to the model
Per classteachers decide where the tutor is switched on

The challenge

An AI tutor that helps without giving the answer away, using only the blocks a child has unlocked.

How we built it

A hint service that works out the gap to the goal before asking the model for a short hint. It is in production: if a safe hint cannot be produced, nothing is shown.

How each hint is made

  1. 1Work out the gap to the goal
  2. 2Ask the model for a short hint
  3. 3Run a safety check on the hint
  4. 4Show nothing if the hint is not safe

08FAQ

Questions we hear before the first call.

How do we start?

A 30-minute call, then an AI Diagnostic or a code audit with a fixed scope and a written outcome.

Who will we work with?

A Product Engineer who runs discovery with your people, writes the spec and owns the backlog and the weekly demo. Agentic Engineers who build with AI agents and own every merge. Specialised agents handle specs, code, tests, reviews and documentation. You always talk to people.

Which models do you use?

The right one per task: Claude, OpenAI-compatible models or local open models. You can choose. An AI gateway lets us change models without touching your applications.

Does our data leave the company?

Not when it must stay. Models can run on your infrastructure, and agents reach your data only through registered tools.

Who owns the code?

You do, on acceptance.

What happens after go-live?

We keep it running, in hours packages or with a dedicated squad.

Do you work with OutSystems and Mendix?

Yes. Our agents work through MCP inside the low-code platforms you already run. See our OutSystems and Mendix practices.

Which sectors have you worked in?

We have AI work delivered or in delivery in retail, customer experience, education, accounting, public sector, insurance, construction, postal and logistics.

Do you work in our time zone?

Yes. Nearshore from Portugal: your time zone, English by default, and teams that start small and grow with you. Offices in Lisboa, Porto, Açores and Castelo Branco.

When will we hear back?

Within one working day of your message.
