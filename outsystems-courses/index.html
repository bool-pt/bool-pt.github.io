05FAQs

Questions we hear often.

Who are the OutSystems bootcamps for?

Developers, architects, tech leads, operations engineers and project managers who work with OutSystems or are about to. Each bootcamp page says who it is targeted at and what you should know beforehand.

Do I need programming experience?

For the ODC Developer, Reactive Developer and Traditional Web Developer bootcamps, some knowledge of relational databases and basic programming concepts is enough. The advanced, mobile, architecture, security and platform ops bootcamps assume previous OutSystems experience.

What is a bootcamp?

An intensive, hands-on course that teaches a lot in a short time. Ours run for one week, 4 or 8 hours a day, in small groups.

Practical focus. You work on exercises and a final project from day one, inside the OutSystems development environment.

Specialized trainers. An OutSystems Certified Trainer leads the group and is available throughout, so nobody gets stuck.

Certification. Every bootcamp ends with a sample test and a free attempt at the official OutSystems certification exam.

Is the training online or in person?

Both. We run bootcamps in our classroom in Lisbon, at your office or fully remote, and a mix of the two also works. For remote sessions you only need your laptop and a stable internet connection.

Is the certification exam included?

Yes. Each bootcamp prepares you for a specific OutSystems certification and we provide the exam voucher, so you can take the exam at no cost.
